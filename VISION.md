# Vision

## Mission

Build world-class data analytics skills through deliberate practice, real-world projects, and professional documentation.


---

## Career Goals

### Short-Term Goals

- Strengthen advanced SQL skills.
- Master Power BI.
- Improve Python for analytics.
- Build a professional portfolio.
- Start freelancing on Upwork.
- Expand into finance and e-commerce analytics.

### Long-Term Goals

Become a Senior Data Analyst at a leading technology, finance, or e-commerce company.

Be recognized for strong technical skills, business thinking, communication, and documentation.

---

## Standards

Every project should:

- Solve a real business problem.
- Be reproducible.
- Be well documented.
- Include business insights.
- Include recommendations.
- Be professional enough to share with recruiters or clients.

---

## Learning Philosophy

Learn.

Document.

Build.

Improve.

Repeat.

## Success Metrics

I will measure success by:

- Skills mastered
- Projects completed
- Business problems solved
- Portfolio quality
- Interview readiness
- Freelance opportunities
- Career growth

—not by the number of courses completed.
